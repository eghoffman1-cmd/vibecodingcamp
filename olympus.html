Ten gods · four move slots each · you write every move

Clash of Olympus

Design the moves for Zeus, Poseidon, Hades and the rest of the pantheon in the Move Forge. Every move costs divine power, so you choose: raw power, accuracy, extra uses or nasty side effects. Then pick a champion and battle the other gods.

Designing moves

  • Each god holds up to 4 moves.
  • A move can cost at most 100 divine power. Strong, accurate, many-use moves cost more.
  • Moves that share their god's element hit 20% harder.
  • Turn on Titan mode in the Forge to remove the limit.

Move types

  • Strike deals damage. Drain deals damage and heals half.
  • Curse only inflicts Burn, Stun, Weaken or Blind.
  • Heal, Shield and Rally (attack boost) target yourself.
  • Swift moves always go first.

Elements

Super effective hits do 1.6×, resisted hits 0.6×.
